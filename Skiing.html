Skiing

Our skiing services are relatively new. We only launched them in 2016 but they have been proven to be massively popular and competitively priced.
We offer five ski slopes from nursery slopes to larger, faster slopes for more advanced skiers. Our skiing services offer value for money for the absolute beginners and advanced skiers and anywhere in between.

Alpine Skiing

Involves skiing on downhill slopes with skis fixed to the boots (using fixed-heel bindings). It focuses on speed, carving turns, and control on groomed, marked trails.

Cross-Country Skiing

Skiing on flat or rolling terrain, where the skis glide with a striding motion. It emphasizes endurance and is often done in tracks or on trails in natural settings.

Freestyle Skiing

Includes skiing on terrain parks with jumps, rails, and halfpipes. Athletes perform tricks, flips, and aerial maneuvers, focusing on creativity and skill in the air.

Backcountry Skiing

Involves skiing off-marked trails in remote, natural areas. Skiers often ascend mountains using climbing skins or hike up before descending, with a focus on exploring and accessing untracked snow.
