Snowboarding

Welcome to Xscapade Snowboarding, the ultimate snowboarding destination for riders of all levels. Our expansive mountain features a variety of terrain, from beginner slopes to challenging black diamond runs. Whether you're new to snowboarding or looking to perfect your tricks, we offer lessons with certified instructors, including private coaching and group sessions. Explore our terrain parks, complete with jumps, rails, and halfpipes, or enjoy scenic rides down untouched powder. On-site rentals, cozy lounges, and a vibrant après-ski scene ensure an unforgettable experience for all.
